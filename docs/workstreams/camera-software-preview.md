## Bounds, private evidence and result authority

The controller reserves ten of sixty total seconds for cleanup; early success does not enlarge that cleanup budget. Consumer20s, daemon/producer30s and link5s deadlines include their startup. Query callbacks receive absolute deadlines and must be nonblocking/bounded. Natural returned exits are observed before output-policy checks. A log/read/schema failure cannot skip stop/reap attempts for the reserved actor. First failure is sticky; cleanup failure and cancellation are independent. Partial startup remains UNKNOWN until an owned PID or explicit positive NO_LAUNCH witness exists. Cleanup-origin exits never become natural EOS or successful transport.

The graph cap is1MiB. The maximum actor stdout/stderr is1MiB each. PNG is2MiB, regular single-link caller-owned0600 under a canonical private0700 directory, with bounded rereads/file and directory identity checks. PNG validation requires exact160×120 noninterlacedRGB8 IHDR, ordered bounded IHDR/IDAT/IEND chunks, CRCs, complete bounded zlib extent, exact decoded scanline length and valid filters. Extra/truncated/compression-bomb data and changed final frame bytes refuse. It validates format/geometry, not image quality or camera origin. Actual-runtime terminal logs and registry bytes must match fixed private files, not only an in-memory assertion.

Only a closed4KiB result is returned: source/grant/graph/frame hashes, geometry and finite actor/exit/EOS/reaping/cleanup facts. No PIDs, paths, command text, raw graph, logs or frame bytes are exported. A controlled backend yields PASS_CONTROLLED_FIXTURE with software transport, physical camera, display and hardware NOT RUN. Future PASS_SOFTWARE requires independent admission and actual backend evidence; it cannot close camera acceptance or imply the Air's firmware/ISP, integrated capture,16K, privacy indicator, application/browser behavior or performance. The caller must additionally bind acceptance to its successful process outcome and disposable-unit cleanup; no postmortem receipt is invented after an uncatchable termination.

## Bounded cancellation correction

The first source hold `8527abe6990b1cc89f13ca8570764ebc19f5198327b6cc63641df13396d9b950` and independent blocked peer `472f8a3c105b6b706f8e9fa5d414820b02ed562f10b69ffd0d071179d64b8998` remain immutable. The read-only review `8dab66fdd0e597edba496a85f4ddcaac409fbe10dd0ba720cd270fa7506b0ae7` identified two concrete source defects: an actual SIGTERM caught during handler restoration could return fixture PASS with cancelled=false, and direct start/cleanup control exceptions did not independently latch cancellation. That hold's21 passing tests did not cover these schedules.

The correction masks SIGINT/SIGTERM before restoring either handler. It attempts both restorations, retries a control-interrupted action at most once, checks the restored handlers and records pending signals. It drains each originally unblocked standard signal at most once using a zero-time wait; an originally blocked caller signal remains pending. The original caller mask is restored. Control errors are remembered independently of earlier ordinary finalization errors. The supervisor rechecks the retained event list after restoration and after reading its candidate projection; Python KeyboardInterrupt/SystemExit during candidate construction/read revoke it before return. All public fields, schema version, status enums and4KiB total cap remain unchanged. There is no durable/public frame publication or new log/path/error-text field.

A first failure CANCELLED now always sets cancelled=true, while earlier GRAPH_REFUSED/other business failures stay first when cancellation occurs later. Cleanup observation, stop, sleep, reap, boundary, final frame and log control exceptions latch cancellation independently, continue scoped teardown and retain the first cleanup cause. The projection refuses CANCELLED-with-false and any cancelled PASS/BLOCKED. A transient failure during handler restoration cannot yield a successful result. Restoration cannot guarantee completion if the underlying API persistently fails; it refuses acceptance.

The guarantee is bounded to catchable control and the retained finalization window. Standard POSIX signals coalesce; the source does not claim a precise signal count. A signal arriving after the last pending check/final unmask can invoke the original handler or terminate the process. A repeated interrupt during the failure projection can propagate and prevent any returned receipt. No authoritative postmortem result or protection after the final custody/return boundary is invented. Actual caller outcome and independent unit cleanup remain required, and no concurrent foreign-writer authenticity is claimed.
